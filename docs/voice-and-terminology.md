# Third Shot voice and terminology

Third Shot sounds calm, direct, and teammate-like. Lead with what happened and
the next useful action. Prefer short sentences and familiar verbs; reserve
playful language for celebrations, never errors or required decisions.

## Core action verbs

- **Start** creates live play now.
- **Join** enters an existing game or group.
- **Schedule** creates play for later.
- **Invite players** sends invitations.
- **Next** advances a multi-step form without committing it.

## Product nouns

- **Open play** means a venue's recurring drop-in program only.
- **Pickup game** means a player-created casual game.
- **Play session** means a larger or recurring player-created gathering.
- **Ranked match** means a confirmed result that changes the **Third Shot match rating**.
- **Self-rating** is the player's editable 2.0–5.5 level. **DUPR** is always
  labeled as an external rating and never shortened to “rating” by itself.

## Privacy and status copy

State the audience and the protected data separately. A court check-in is
visible to signed-in players viewing that court; precise device coordinates
remain private. Never call the whole check-in private.

Errors use the pattern “what happened + what to do next,” without jokes,
blame, or implementation codes. Success copy may be warm, but should still say
exactly what was saved, sent, joined, or scheduled.
