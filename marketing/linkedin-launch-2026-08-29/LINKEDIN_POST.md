# Third Shot LinkedIn launch post

## Ready-to-paste post

I just pushed a big Third Shot update live.

A lot of pickleball apps help you find a court. I wanted Third Shot to help you actually get four people on it.

Getting a game together should not take 30 texts, three group chats, and a pile of “maybe” replies. So I rebuilt the whole local-game loop.

Now you can:

• Tap Play now when you’re already at the court and ready  
• Mark yourself Available this hour when you could play nearby  
• Tap I’m on my way for directions and a real 5, 10, or 15-minute held spot  
• Plan a game ahead with the people you want  
• Use Fill the game to invite friends, ask the court, or share one live link  
• Join or waitlist straight from the live court card  
• Keep a good group together as a private Crew, then plan the next game or tap Play again now  
• Search 18,000+ courts on a map that works without an API key

The live card follows the actual roster. It fills when the game fills, reopens if somebody drops, and closes with the game. No stale “we need one more” post sitting around all day.

It’s live now: https://third-shot.vercel.app

It’s still early. If you play pickleball, try it at your local court and tell me exactly where you get stuck. I genuinely want the feedback.
