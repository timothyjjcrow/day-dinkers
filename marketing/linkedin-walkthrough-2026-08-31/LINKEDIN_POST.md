# Third Shot — pickleball business partnership post

## Ready-to-paste LinkedIn post

If you run a pickleball business, I’d love to talk.

Players don’t want to jump between a bunch of apps.

They want to find a court, see what’s happening, get a game together and play.

That’s the journey we’re building with Third Shot.

Now we’re looking for businesses that want to integrate with Third Shot:

• Clubs and facilities  
• Court-booking platforms  
• Tournament and league operators  
• Coaches and clinic organizers  
• Pickleball brands and technology partners  

Together, we could connect court availability, reservations, open-play listings, events, registrations, memberships, lessons and more.

These are partnership opportunities, not a list of integrations already live today. We want to build them thoughtfully with the right businesses.

If your business helps people play more pickleball, send me a DM. I’d genuinely like to hear what your business needs and how we could work together.

Explore Third Shot: https://third-shot.vercel.app
